HOW TO PLAY

The tournament has 4 rounds:

🥊 Classic: 100 points for a right answer, plus up to 50 bonus points for speed. Miss it and the next player or team can steal for 50.

✖️2 Double Points: everything is worth double, including steals.

⚡ Lightning: only 8 seconds, and no steals.

🎲 Final Wager: bet part or all of your points on one last question. A right answer adds the bet and a wrong one takes it away.

Teams: split into 2 to 4 teams. Captains take turns answering for the team, so everyone gets a turn on the hot seat.

Crew Questions: add your own “Who on the crew…” questions. They get mixed into the next tournament.

Earn badges along the way and claim your spot in the Hall of Champions!

CREW QUESTIONS

Write your own questions, like “Who on the crew has been here the longest?”
